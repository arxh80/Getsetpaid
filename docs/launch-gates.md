# Launch gates

Phase 10 (launch hardening) measures every launch gate against the evidence
that exists for it. The PRD's exit criterion for the phase is "All launch gates
passed", and its success metric is "zero known critical tenant-isolation
vulnerabilities at launch; all high-severity findings remediated".

This document is the source Part B of the Phase 10 plan
(`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-16-launch-hardening.md`) is written from. Each
gap below carries a proposed remediation and a proposed test, and Part B turns
them into tasks.

**Written 2026-10-17 (Phase 10 Task 1) at branch `launch-hardening`, base
`af69ee4`**, stacked on `q228-closure-backup-parts` (PR #32). The status
vocabulary:

- `MET`: the evidence exists and was measured green.
- `PARTIAL`: some evidence exists, with named gaps.
- `OPEN`: there is no evidence.
- `FOUNDER ACTION`: an agent cannot do it and must never simulate it.

**The census's hand-read results, in brief:**

- **Routes.**
  - 43 routes are really scanned by axe. Three of them (`/account/privacy`,
    `/account/data-requests` and the network profile editor) are reached by a
    click, so the regex cannot see them.
  - 2 routes are not UI: `/` only redirects, and `/super/[...unknown]` calls
    `notFound()` and renders the in-app 404 that `accessibility.spec.ts`
    scans.
  - **42 UI routes are scanned by nothing.** The census's "exact" verdict also
    over-reports. A `/new` literal fills a `[id]` segment, so `artists/[id]`,
    `companies/[id]` and `venues/[id]` look covered. Some specs navigate to a
    page without scanning it: `contacts/[id]/edit`, `/invites` and `venues`.
- **List reads.** 24 of the 32 candidates are false positives:
  - a bound added in a later statement;
  - a `;` inside a comment, which cuts the statement short;
  - `.maybeSingle<T>(`, which the regex does not recognise;
  - an `.in()` over one page's ids;
  - the caller's own memberships.

  8 are confirmed. Six grow with custom-field definitions (G5) and two with a
  workspace's own published network profiles (G6).
- **Rate limits.** Of the 31 keys:
  - 6 are real `rate_limit_policies` categories;
  - 1, `sign_in_sign_ups`, is GoTrue's per-IP limit in `config.toml`, and no
    test names it (G10);
  - 24 are column, role, extension, alert-type or SQL names that the regex
    picked up.

  § 5 names the other 6 categories in prose, not backticks. All 12
  categories have a test.
- **Definers.** All 107 unnamed definers are unreachable by a client:
  - 41 are trigger functions;
  - 30 are granted to no client role, not even `service_role`;
  - 34 sit in `private` with `authenticated` EXECUTE, so that RLS policies and
    SECURITY INVOKER RPCs can call them as the caller. PostgREST does not
    expose `private`: `config.toml` sets `[api] schemas = public,
    graphql_public`, and the matrix asserts it;
  - `public.hook_password_verification_attempt` is executable by
    `supabase_auth_admin` only;
  - `private.integration_role_held` is not a definer. `20261030002000`
    changed it with `alter function ... security invoker`, and the census
    reads `create function` statements only.

  Across all 189 live definers, every one sets `search_path=""` (G25 asks for
  that to be asserted) and none is anon-executable.

## Gaps, graded

Severity follows the Phase 10 rubric:

- **Critical:** a demonstrated or plausible tenant breakout, private/network
  leak, auth bypass or data loss.
- **High:** a security control promised in `CLAUDE.md`/`SECURITY.md` that is
  missing, or untested where a mutation would go unnoticed.
- **Medium:** coverage missing on a control that is otherwise tested
  elsewhere.
- **Low:** documentation or ergonomics.

Where a gap needs a founder decision, it says so. The decision is a founder
question in `task-1-report.md` with a proposed default.
